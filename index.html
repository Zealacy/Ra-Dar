All SNOTEL numbers

Snow depth can wobble by about an inch from hour to hour. SWE is snow water equivalent. Density is ΔSWE ÷ Δdepth: under 7% is light, dry powder, 7–12% is typical, and over 12% is heavy, wet snow.
