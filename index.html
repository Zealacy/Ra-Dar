Measured · NRCS SNOTEL

Automated federal snow stations near the ski area, reporting hourly. They sit lower than the base (5,700 ft or below), so treat them as a minimum for what fell on the mountain.

Last 7 days at

Snow depth from the sonic sensor can wobble by about an inch from hour to hour. Snow water equivalent (SWE) comes from a snow pillow that weighs the snowpack. New-snow density is ΔSWE ÷ Δdepth: under 7% is light, dry powder, 7–12% is typical, and over 12% is heavy, wet snow.
